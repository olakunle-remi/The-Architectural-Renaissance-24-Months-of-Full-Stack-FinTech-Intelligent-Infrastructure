* Cloud Engineer
* AI Engineer
* Solutions Architect
* Technical Lead

For your specific goal of getting a remote dollar-paying role as quickly as possible, I would spend **70% of my effort in the first 12 months on Java/Spring Boot, PostgreSQL, React, Docker, AWS, and ISO 8583/jPOS**, because those skills are likely to generate job opportunities sooner while the later quarters build you toward architect-level expertise.
